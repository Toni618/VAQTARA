## 1. Introduction
RAVENDORA ("we", "our") provides productivity, planning, and scheduling services. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how we use/share/protect it, and your rights.

## 2. Data we collect
- Account data: username, optional email, display name, authentication credentials (stored as secure hashes, never plaintext).
- Profile/onboarding data you provide: preferences, schedules, goals, habits, study/work information (optional where stated).
- Usage and device/session data: app interactions, session identifiers, device/session metadata for security (minimized; no unnecessary invasive fingerprinting).
- Subscription/payment data: plan status, transaction identifiers and verification results from payment providers (we do not store full card numbers; providers process payments).
- Support communications.

## 3. How we use data
- Provide and personalize the service (planning, reminders, analytics you request).
- Authenticate users, enforce 24h trial and subscriptions server-side, prevent abuse/fraud, and secure accounts.
- Operate, improve, and troubleshoot the service; comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing
- Service providers (hosting, analytics if enabled, payment processors/app stores) under contractual safeguards.
- Legal/safety requirements when necessary. We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Retention & deletion
We retain data as needed for service and legal purposes. Users may request export/deletion subject to verification and legal requirements.

## 6. Security
